N° 13 · Slovenia

Jesenice.

HDD Jesenice · Alps Hockey League · Slovenia

The Place

A working steel town nicknamed "The Town of Steel and Daffodils" — improbably positioned between Lake Bled and the Julian Alps, with one of the loudest hockey crowds in Europe.

Jesenice itself is industrial. It has been a steel-making town since the 14th century — the rolling mills are still the town's largest employer — and unlike the postcard villages elsewhere on this list, the town centre is concrete and Yugoslav-era apartment blocks. But its setting is the reason it earns a place here: Lake Bled is fifteen minutes away, the Julian Alps and Triglav National Park rise immediately to the south, and the Karawanks form the Austrian border to the north. Springtime brings sheets of wild daffodils to the slopes of nearby Planina pod Golico — hence the nickname.

HDD Jesenice's home rink, Podmežakla Hall, regularly draws crowds of 4,500 — extraordinary for a town of 13,000 — and the "Eternal Derby" against Olimpija Ljubljana is the loudest game in Slovenian sport. The current HDD entity was founded in 2014 after the previous club's bankruptcy, and has since won four Slovenian championships and the 2023 Alps Hockey League title. Ljubljana — one of Europe's most underrated capitals — is forty-five minutes south. English is excellent across Slovenia.

The Team

HDD Jesenice

Founded 2014, after the bankruptcy of the historic HK Jesenice. Has since won four Slovenian championships (2015, 2017, 2018, 2021) and the 2023 Alps Hockey League playoffs — a remarkable record for a club just over a decade old. Nicknamed Železarji, "the Steelworkers."
