The Place

A high plateau above Bolzano reached by a four-kilometre cable car — and a hockey rink built on the infield of a 400-metre outdoor speed-skating oval.

Ritten — Renon in Italian — is a sun-trap of pine forests and alpine meadows perched at 1,150 to 1,400 metres directly above Bolzano. The Renon cable car, one of the longest single-rope tramways on earth, climbs from the city in twelve minutes and replaced a rack railway built in 1907. From the plateau, the view sweeps across to the Latemar and the Rosengarten Dolomites.

The plateau's strangest feature: the earth pyramids near Lengstein and Oberbozen — towering clay spires capped with boulders, formed by glacial mud. The Arena Ritten ice complex, where Rittner Buam play, is the only stadium of its kind in European hockey: the rink sits inside a 400-metre speed-skating oval that has hosted European Championships in 2007 and 2011. As an import, you live up the cable car from a real city — bars and bookshops 12 minutes away — but wake up on a mountain.

The Team

Rittner Buam SkyAlps

The "Buam" — Tyrolean for "boys" — was founded in 1984 from the older 1928 Sport Club Renon. Italian Serie A2 champions across multiple seasons before Italian club hockey was reorganised; now competes year-round in the Alps Hockey League. The unique 1,200-seat venue is the only place in Italy where a hockey rink sits within a full speed-skating oval.
