The Place

A Ladin-speaking village of 2,600 souls beneath the jagged Sassolungo, where Italian, German, and a Romance language older than both share the same valley.

Selva sits at the head of Val Gardena in South Tyrol, at 1,563 metres, beneath the white spires of the Sella and Sassolungo massifs. Nearly nine in ten residents speak Ladin as their first language — a Rhaeto-Romance tongue that pre-dates the Italian and German that surround it. Italian and German are also official; the village name appears in all three on most signs (Sëlva · Wolkenstein · Selva di Val Gardena).

The village functions as the Italian launch point for the Sella Ronda, the legendary 26-kilometre circuit around the Sella massif. HC Gherdëina plays at the Pranives ice stadium right in the village centre, founded as a club in 1927 and still owned by the community. As an alpine import experience, this is about as authentic and unhurried as European hockey gets.

The Team

HC Gherdëina Valgardena.it

Founded 1927. Won the Italian championship four times — 1969, 1976, 1980, 1981 — back when Val Gardena was a small-town giant of Italian hockey. Now plays in the Alps Hockey League at a 2,000-seat rink in the village centre.
