Kitzbühel.

EC Adler Stadtwerke Kitzbühel · Alps Hockey League · Austria

The Place

A medieval Tyrolean town and one of the most exclusive ski resorts in the world, home to the Hahnenkamm — the hardest downhill course in skiing.

Kitzbühel preserves a complete medieval old town: pastel facades, two cobbled main streets — Vorderstadt and Hinterstadt — and the late-Gothic St Andreas parish church. It sits in a wide valley at 762 metres in the Kitzbühel Alps, low enough to feel like a town rather than a resort, high enough to give clean ski seasons from December into April.

Every January the town hosts the Hahnenkamm races on the Streif slope, widely considered the most dangerous downhill course in alpine skiing. International high society descends; the rest of the year the town is calmer but still distinctly upmarket. Munich is two hours by train, Salzburg the same. The hockey club, EC Kitzbühel — playing as Die Adler ("the Eagles") — is one of the oldest in Austria, founded in 1910.

The Team

EC Adler Stadtwerke Kitzbühel

Founded in 1910, one of Austrian hockey's oldest clubs. Joined the Alps Hockey League for the 2016–17 season. Multiple Austrian Oberliga titles in the modern era (1976, 1996, 2004, 2005). Plays at the Sportpark, completed 2006.
