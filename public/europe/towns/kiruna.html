N° 10 · Swedish Lapland

Kiruna.

Kiruna IF · HockeyEttan Norra · Sweden

The Place

Sweden's northernmost town, 145 km above the Arctic Circle, currently being moved three kilometres east because the iron mine beneath it is too valuable to stop.

Kiruna is one of the strangest urban projects on Earth right now. The Kiirunavaara iron mine — the largest underground iron mine in the world — has been the town's reason for existing since 1899. As the ore body has been worked, the ground above it has begun to deform, and so the entire town centre is being relocated three kilometres east, building by building, since 2014. The old town hall has already been replaced; the historic wooden church (voted Sweden's most beautiful building in 2001) is scheduled to make the journey itself.

The setting is otherworldly: tundra, low birch forest, and the open peaks of Sápmi (Sami Land). The Icehotel — rebuilt every winter from blocks of the Torne River — is fifteen minutes east in Jukkasjärvi. Abisko National Park, considered the best place in the world to see the aurora borealis, is an hour west. Kiruna IF play at the Lombiahallen and were Sweden's first LGBT-certified sports club. English is universal; direct flights connect to Stockholm.

The Team

Kiruna IF

Founded 1988 from the merger of the hockey sections of Kiruna AIF and IFK Kiruna. Voluntarily withdrew from Allsvenskan after 2002–03 for financial reasons; has competed in HockeyEttan Norra since. Made international news in 2014 by playing the season in rainbow jerseys — Sweden's first LGBT-certified sports club.
