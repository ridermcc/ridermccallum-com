N° 07 · French Alps

Morzine.

Pingouins de Morzine-Avoriaz · France Division 1 · France

The Place

A working Savoyard slate-quarry village that became the gateway to Portes du Soleil — and an unlikely outpost of British Alpine life.

Morzine sits in a river gorge on the French side of the Swiss border, at roughly 1,000 metres. Until the late 19th century the local economy was slate quarrying — the dark stone roofs that dominate the village are the original product. Tourism arrived around 1930, and Morzine grew slowly enough to keep its village character: real shops, working farms in the surrounding pastures, a parish church that anchors the centre.

The reason imports come here: Portes du Soleil, the linked ski domain that connects Morzine to Avoriaz, Châtel, and the Swiss resorts of Champéry and Les Crosets — 600 kilometres of pistes across the border. The Anglo seasonnaire community has put down deep roots; English is the second language of half the bars, and the Pingouins de Morzine-Avoriaz have a roster pipeline that runs from Geneva (90 minutes away) to North America.
