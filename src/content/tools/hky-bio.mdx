## What it is

A platform specifically designed for hockey players, and eventually teams, leagues, coaches, and advisors. 

It offers one central location to manage your online hockey presence, including social links like profiles (ep, instat, etc), Instagram, and other social media elements. 

The platform allows you to share a single link that has your entire story, from personal branding to statistics, articles, highlights, and schedule information.

## Who it's for

Any hockey player who wants a clean, shareable profile they have control over. 

It was also built to accomodate the growing demand for managing your personal brand as a player. 

As you join the growing number of players sharing what they do on social media, hky.bio allows you to portray your expertise and direct your audience where you want them to go.

## Why I built it

There's an influx in bad sports platforms that all try to do the same thing: monetize the likeness of individual players while giving the player zero control over how they are portrayed online. 

I wanted a place to show my game, share what I'm building away from hockey, and progress my career on my own terms. hky.bio is a free platform where players have control over how they are seen as athletes, and more importantly, people.

You can check it out [here.](https://hky.bio/)
