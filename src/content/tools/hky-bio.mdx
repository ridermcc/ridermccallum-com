## What it is

A link-in-bio platform designed specifically for hockey players. One URL that holds everything: social links, career stats, articles, game schedules, and highlights. Built so players can share one link instead of juggling five.

## Who it's for

Any hockey player who wants a clean, shareable profile page. Works for juniors trying to get recruited, college players building a presence, and pros who want something better than a Linktree.

## How it works

Players sign up, fill out their profile, and get a shareable URL. Stats pull in automatically, articles are scraped from tagged sources, and the layout is built around what hockey people actually need — not generic social templates.

## Why I built it

I wanted a single page I could send to coaches, scouts, and media that had everything in one place. Nothing like that existed for hockey, so I built it.
