## Plymouth State

When the time came to move on from junior hockey, I felt uncertain about my path foward. It wasn't until a conversation with Coach Russell from Plymouth State that I got the gut feeling I'd been waiting for.

My decision to become a Panther was driven by an opportunity to continue the pursuit of my hockey career. The environment and lifestyle were somewhat of an afterthougt. Upon arriving in the fall of 2022, I realized this would become my driving force for the love I hold for this place in my heart.

The people, the mountains, and the slower pace of life in central NH made this move a welcoming transition. As my connection within the community grew over time, I got to share many adventures with many great people.

## Hockey

Two conference championships, among many other special memories with the family.

<img
  src="/notes/grad-2026/the-team/conference-champions.jpg"
  alt="Conference championsssss"
/>

Coming in as a freshman, Plymouth had been a conference powerhouse winning almost every year for a long time. A national tournament win just hadn't been realised yet. We set out to change that. Getting stunned at home my first year, we got over the hump and won the first national tournament game in school history the following year. This may not seem like much. In the end I'm not a national champion. I failed in leading the team to one in the following years. But we all grew. We all exprienced euphoria and heartbreak together as a family. This one win. This slice of history within the program is a special feeling that that team will hold memories of forever.

I got to be a part of two conference championships, among some other awesome milestones, with 30 of my closest people. It was special to be in a unified journey of progression and growth with these guys. 

## The place

Plymouth reminded me of home in Revelstoke, BC. A small town tucked into the mountains, where you walk everywhere and recognize half the faces on the way. The pace was slower than I was used to. That ended up being the part I needed most. Good people, good food, and enough quiet to figure a few things out.
