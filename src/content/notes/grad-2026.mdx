## Plymouth State

I came here to play hockey for a team that mattered to a community. Hockey is my gateway to making an impact on people and Plymouth kept that gateway open.

The thing about a small school in a small town is that you can't hide. Everyone knows everyone and everything. Some people love it. Some people hate it. I quickly learned to love it.

PSU tied me to many great people and many grand adventures.
