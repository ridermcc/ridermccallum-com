My goal is to help athletes use technology to build useful things.

The barrier to entry for creating software is dropping rapidly.

Athletes have a unique perspective that the tech world hasn't seen much of yet.

We can create tools that have an impact.

I want to share what I’m building in case you can learn from it.

The plan: build stuff, share it, and make it easy for the next person to adapt it for them.

I built this site alongside [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) and the full repo is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ridermccallum/ridermccallum-com).

Two kinds of posts will live here:

- **Tools** I build and share.
- **Notes** on what I'm learning/doing.

Let's build. The first thing I did is [hky.bio](/tools/hky-bio).
